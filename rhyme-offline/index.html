Rhyme Offline

A rhyming dictionary app with perfect rhymes, slant rhymes, definitions, a thesaurus, related words, and more! It works fully offline, including in airplane mode, and is free with no ads!

Why did I make Rhyme Offline?

I got the idea for Rhyme Offline while writing lyrics on a flight. I downloaded some rhyming dictionary apps in preparation, but found them to be incredibly limiting, with many not working in airplane mode, or lacking features like near rhymes. Anything more comprehensive cost money, sometimes even subscriptions. I quickly learned other songwriter friends had similar challenges, whether it be no offline support, or hopping between apps for other features like synonyms. I've built Rhyme Offline to not only be the best offline rhyming dictionary app, but the most comprehensive tool available, with or without internet access, paid or free.
